About

I practise a simple way of being mindful that I call Universe Watching. You notice the world around you as it happens, sometimes relaxed and sometimes focused, and let it settle into quiet meditation. I do it every day on my mindfulness runs, and I'm now sharing it with beginners.

What I teach

  1. Watch: notice sights, sounds and feelings without judging them.
  2. Balance: switch between relaxed noticing and gentle focus.
  3. Settle: let it turn into a few minutes of still meditation.
