Mindfulness (stepping into teaching)

I practise a simple way of being mindful that I call Universe Watching. You notice the world around you as it happens, sometimes relaxed and sometimes focused, and let it settle into quiet meditation. I've done it daily on my mindfulness runs since 2024 and am now offering beginner sessions online over Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp.

  1. Watch: notice sights, sounds and feelings without judging them.
  2. Balance: switch between relaxed noticing and gentle focus.
  3. Settle: let it turn into a few minutes of still meditation.
